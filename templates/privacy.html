Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and password. When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to save your expenses, and to show you summaries and spending breakdowns. We do not sell your personal data.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can request deletion of your account and its data by contacting us.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services for hosting, infrastructure, or fonts. These providers only receive the information needed to deliver their service, and their own privacy policies apply to that data.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to the Spendly team through the contact details published on the site.
